A simple production path

Keep your assets organized and your final video editable. Start with still images if video generation tools are limited.

01 / Generate the visuals

  1. Use the scene prompts to generate five 9:16 images with a tool you can access.
  2. Keep the same product reference image, bottle shape, cap, label placement, and lighting palette.
  3. Generate clean images without text when possible. Add the exact copy later in Canva.
  4. If a tool offers free credits, check limits, export resolution, watermark rules, and commercial-use terms before publishing.

02 / Build the storyboard sheet

  1. Create a 1920 × 1080 landscape design in Canva if custom dimensions are available on your account.
  2. Place five equal panels in a horizontal row if legible; otherwise use a 3-panel top row and 2-panel bottom row.
  3. Each panel: image, frame number, timecode, action, camera direction, and on-screen text.
  4. Keep headings, timecodes, and notes as editable text elements. Use black, warm ivory, and champagne gold consistently.

03 / Assemble the vertical ad

  1. Create a 1080 × 1920 vertical video design (9:16).
  2. Import five images and place them in order, each with a 3-second duration.
  3. Add editable text overlays using the exact copy below each scene. Keep text away from app interface areas at the top and bottom.
  4. Use gentle pan/zoom, short dissolves, and one cohesive music bed. Preview the complete timeline.
  5. Export MP4 at the best available quality. Reopen the exported file and confirm runtime is exactly 00:15.

04 / Free-access tools: verify first

  • Canva Free: editing and simple motion; features, stock assets, and export options can vary.
  • AI image generators: useful for still frames; free tiers may have quotas, queues, or restrictions.
  • Image-to-video tools: free access may mean limited credits or preview-only output, not unlimited downloads.
  • Audio: look for music and effects with explicit commercial-use permission; save the license and attribution details.

Do not assume “free to use” means commercial use is permitted. Confirm the terms for your account and the exact asset.
